## seated-press-edited.png

Imagen de entrada: `seated-press.jpg`.

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: supplied manufacturer product photo of a seated chest press gym machine. Remove only the floating promotional badge at the upper right and reconstruct the plain white background and any small underlying area of the machine accurately. Keep the entire machine exactly as in the photograph: identical geometry, mechanisms, framing, black upholstery, materials, logos physically printed on the machine, and lighting. Clean white product photography for a sage green minimal fitness app. No new text, no new objects, no background scene. Output a square image with the full machine visible and comfortable margins. Save the edited result as a local image asset.
